Pixelate

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-31. Applies to the iOS app “Pixelate: Guess the Picture” by Taisen Industries LLC.

The short version

There are no accounts. Your progress stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers to serve and measure ads; you choose whether it may track you. Purchases go through Apple. We never see your name, email or payment details.

What stays on your device

  • Your results, stats, streak, streak freezes and which purchases you own are saved in the app’s storage on your iPhone or iPad.
  • We have no server that receives this data. If you use iCloud or computer backups, iOS may include it in your device backup, under your Apple account and Apple’s terms.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve, cap and measure ads, Google’s SDK collects information such as your IP address, device model and OS version, ad interactions, and an advertising identifier.
  • iOS asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, Google may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalised ads. If you decline, the IDFA is not shared and the ads you see are less personalised.
  • Ad installs and conversions may be measured with Apple’s SKAdNetwork, which reports results without identifying you.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. You can also manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center.
  • Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases (Apple)

Purchases and restores are handled entirely by Apple. Your payment details never reach the app or us. The app receives only a confirmation of which items you own, which it stores on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device. No push service is used, and no notification data is sent to us.

The daily schedule file

When it opens, the app downloads a small file, puzzles.json, from this website to check for schedule updates. The request contains no personal data from the app. As with any web request, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address and may log it under its own policy.

Sharing your result

Sharing uses the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. If you save a result image, iOS asks for permission to add it to your Photos; the app cannot read your photo library.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, logins, or sign-in with any service.
  • No analytics SDK of our own, no location access, no contacts, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: support@taisenindustries.com.
