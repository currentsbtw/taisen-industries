When does the next puzzle unlock?

At midnight in your local time zone. Everyone gets the same picture on the same calendar day.

How does the streak work?

Your streak grows by one each day you solve the puzzle. A failed puzzle does not reset it; only a day you don’t play at all does. Past puzzles from the archive count toward your stats but not your streak.

What is a streak freeze?

You earn one streak freeze for every 7 days in a row, and you can bank up to 2. If you miss a day, a banked freeze is used automatically and your streak survives.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the in-app store and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple account you bought with. Purchases are tied to your Apple account, so they carry over to a new device.

How do I turn off the daily reminder?

Go to Settings › Notifications › Pixelate on your iPhone and turn off Allow Notifications.

How do I stop ad tracking?

Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn it off for Pixelate. See the privacy policy for details.

I got a new phone. Is my streak gone?

Progress is stored on your device. If you restore your new phone from an iCloud or computer backup, your progress comes with it.
