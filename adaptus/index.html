Adaptus: NCLEX Prep

Adaptus is a focused NCLEX-RN prep app built around one idea: practice on questions pitched to your level, and see honestly how ready you are. It pairs 4,000+ NCLEX-style questions — each with a clear explanation — with an adaptive engine that targets your weak spots, Next Generation NCLEX case studies, and a readiness score that forecasts your likelihood of passing as you study.

Coming soon to the App Store

What you get

  • 4,000+ NCLEX-style questions with clear explanations, across every major nursing domain — med-surg, pharmacology, maternity, pediatrics, mental health, fundamentals, and more.
  • An adaptive engine that estimates your ability from every answer and steers you toward the questions that move your score.
  • A readiness gauge that forecasts your likelihood of passing as you study.
  • Next Generation NCLEX (NGN) case studies and the newer item formats you’ll see on today’s exam.
  • Daily drills, boot-camp sessions, and spaced-repetition flashcards — unlocked with a one-time purchase, no subscription.
